1. Purpose and boundaries

 This document defines the Lifestyle feature that follows Vitals in the screening workflow. It is the reference Luna must read
 before implementation prompts are executed. The feature collects analyzable alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, physical-activity,
 work, and other-activity information while remaining fast, respectful, offline capable, and non-diagnostic.

 Objectives

q Collect quantitative weekly data for alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, and physical activity.
q Collect structured plus descriptive occupation and other-activity context.
q Minimize visible questions through conditional branching and collapsible cards.
q Create a stable baseline profile and immutable baseline history without rewriting earlier encounters.
q Support Save Draft, recovery, and Continue using the application's established security and transactional boundaries.
q Prepare clean domain data for later reporting and FHIR mapping without implementing either in this feature.

 Out of scope

q Diagnosis of alcohol-use disorder, nicotine dependence, or physical inactivity.
q Automated clinical recommendations, referrals, printing, reporting, remote sync, or FHIR persistence.
q Changes to authentication, Patient Search, session creation, encounter creation, or Vitals behavior.
q Food and over-the-counter medication questionnaires; they are separate later modules.

 Clinical posture

 The module is a screening and data-collection tool. It may calculate transparent totals and validated questionnaire scores
 when separately approved, but it must not attach stigmatizing labels or substitute for clinical assessment.

the selected screening period, normally the past seven days.

Baseline versioning rule

describe the action without copying clinical answers.

6. Physical activity design

Use "Physical Activity," not only "Exercise," because farming, domestic work, construction, and walking may be the patient's
primary activity. The weekly record separates domains so totals remain interpretable.

Weekly entry question

During the past 7 days, did you perform physical activity for at least 10 continuous minutes?
Options: Yes / No / Unable to answer / Prefer not to answer. The 10-minute threshold must remain configurable if the
approved protocol changes.

 If Yes: select domains

q Work or farming
q Walking or cycling for transportation
q Household or caregiving activity
q Exercise, sport, or recreation

Fields per selected domain/activity row

Field                                               Type                         Rule
Activity category                                   Controlled value             Must belong to selected domain.
Description                                         Short optional text          Useful for local activities.
Intensity                                           Light / moderate / vigorous  Display plain-language examples.
Days in past 7 days                                 Integer                      1-7.
Average minutes per active day                      Integer                      Positive, realistic configured maximum.
Weekly minutes                                      Calculated                   Days x minutes; retain inputs.

Sedentary time

Collect usual sitting or reclining time per day, excluding sleep, using hours and minutes or total minutes. Unknown and
declined remain distinct. Do not subtract sedentary time from activity totals.

 Calculation policy

q Store raw days, minutes, and intensity, then calculate totals deterministically.
q Retain moderate and vigorous minutes separately; do not silently convert them unless a documented derived metric is

     added.
q Prevent double entry by reminding the screener not to repeat an activity already captured in another domain.
q Calculated totals support display and later analysis; they do not create a diagnosis.

Weekly work question

Did you perform your usual work during the past 7 days?
Yes - usual amount / Yes - less than usual / Yes - more than usual / No / Not applicable / Unknown / Prefer not to answer. If
the occupation changed, the screener opens Work Baseline and creates a new version.

Other activity

Ask: "Did you perform any other regular physical activity during the past 7 days that was not already recorded?" If Yes,
capture category, description, days, minutes per day, and intensity. Categories include farming/gardening, household work,
caregiving, religious/community activity, walking for errands, sport, and Other.

Duplication guard

The application may warn about similar descriptions or identical category/day/minute combinations, but must not delete or
merge entries automatically. The screener decides whether the activity is a duplicate.

 Data invariants

q One Lifestyle draft per encounter; patient, encounter, session, location, and installation attribution is authoritative and
     immutable.

q Renderer never supplies actor, version authority, timestamps, baseline history authority, calculated scores, session,
     location, or installation.

q Stable child IDs support add/update/remove reconciliation; cross-draft and duplicate IDs are rejected.
q Blank, zero, No, Unknown, Not applicable, and Prefer not to answer are distinct states.
q Existing created timestamps are preserved; unchanged child rows must not receive artificial updated timestamps.
q A completed weekly record stores the baseline-version references used for interpretation.

 Suggested controlled codes

 Use internal enums or controlled codes rather than UI labels for status, response, product, unit, activity domain, intensity,
 employment status, and shift pattern. Labels may later be localized without changing stored meaning.

 Cross-field validation examples

q Alcohol drinking days cannot be zero when weekly use is Yes.
q Largest one-day alcohol amount cannot exceed total weekly drinks.
q Days at largest amount cannot exceed drinking days.
q A tobacco product row requires product, days, quantity, and unit.
q Activity days and minutes must be positive when an activity row exists.
q No weekly use must not coexist with positive quantities.
q Former/Never conflict is a review requirement, not an automatic clinical conclusion.
q Text inputs are trimmed, normalized under existing conventions, and length-limited.

 Recovery and lifecycle

 Lifestyle must follow the already approved recovery pattern: an existing editable draft may be restored after logout/login,
 restart, or date rollover without rewriting historical attribution. First-draft creation must use the current encounter/session
 authority defined by the established workflow. Closed or non-editable encounters remain blocked.

10. Offline-first behavior and transactions

 All feature behavior must work without network access. The local database is the immediate system of record for the desktop
 workflow. This design does not add remote sync.

 Save transaction

q Validate authenticated actor, permission, trusted IPC sender, installation, patient, encounter ownership, and lifecycle.
q Load authoritative current draft, baseline versions, child records, and optimistic version.
q Validate baseline/weekly branching and normalized inputs.
q Insert or version approved baseline changes without overwriting earlier versions.
q Reconcile weekly child rows by stable ID, skipping updates for unchanged rows.
q Update the parent draft/version under the established clock.
q Write approved sanitized audit and outbox records.
q Return success only after commit; any failure rolls back every change.

 Concurrency

 A stale renderer version returns the established sanitized conflict result. No automatic last-write-wins behavior is permitted.
 Baseline updates and weekly saves that depend on them must be coordinated transactionally or through an explicitly
 designed version contract.

 Future sync preparation

 Use stable identifiers, version numbers, created/updated timestamps, tombstone or approved deletion semantics, and
 immutable encounter attribution consistent with the project architecture. Do not create network calls, remote identifiers,
 merge algorithms, or FHIR resources in this task.

11. Security, privacy, and accessibility

 Security boundaries

q All authorization and persistence remain in the main process; renderer access is through narrow validated IPC contracts.
q Renderer input cannot assert actor, role, patient ownership, installation, location, session, encounter lifecycle, timestamps,

     audit metadata, baseline status history, or calculated authority.
q Do not log clinical answers, patient identifiers, notes, raw database errors, SQL, or full IPC payloads.
q Return stable sanitized result codes and user-safe messages.
q Do not introduce bypass, force, recovery, admin, or override flags.

 Privacy-sensitive interaction

q Use neutral wording and a visible "Prefer not to answer" option where approved.
q Do not display stigmatizing labels such as alcoholic, addict, or inactive.
q Keep baseline summaries concise so sensitive detail is not unnecessarily exposed on screen.
q Do not prefill a new week as if prior behavior occurred again; prior answers may be shown only as clearly labeled

     reference.

 Accessibility

q Every input has a programmatic label; required state is not conveyed by color alone.
q Expandable panels expose expanded/collapsed state to assistive technology.
q Validation summaries link to fields; focus moves predictably.
q Touch/click targets, contrast, zoom, and supported desktop window sizes follow existing UI standards.
q Quantities include units in labels and errors; examples do not replace labels.

12. Testing strategy

 Unit and domain tests

q Every baseline status and weekly Yes/No/Unknown/Declined branch.
q Alcohol and activity calculations, boundaries, and cross-field contradictions.
q Tobacco product and activity stable-ID reconciliation, duplicates, removal, reordering, and cross-draft rejection.
q Baseline version creation, prior-version preservation, and weekly version references.
q Unchanged-row timestamp preservation and deterministic injected-clock behavior.

 Application and persistence integration

q First draft, update, Save Draft, Continue, optimistic conflict, and rollback.
q Actor/permission/trusted-sender, patient/encounter/installation ownership, and lifecycle rejection.
q Existing-draft recovery across logout/login, restart, and deployment-local date rollover.
q No partial baseline or weekly persistence after a failure.
q Audit/outbox contains no clinical values and only emits success after commit.

 Renderer and UX tests

q Only the required active fields are visible; irrelevant branches are absent.
q Baseline button expands/collapses inline; successful save closes and updates summary.
q Current, former, never, missing, unknown, and declined paths render correctly.
q Former/Never + weekly Yes preserves weekly answers and requires baseline review.
q Completed cards collapse, remain reopenable, and show accessible state.
q Save Draft does not navigate; Continue persists before navigation.
q Keyboard, focus, error announcement, required indicators, and responsive desktop layout.

 Regression gates

 Run repository formatting, lint, type checking, complete tests, verification, build, migration checks, diff checks, and a safe
 desktop smoke test. P0-P4, authentication, Patient Search, sessions, encounters, Vitals, audit, outbox, and recovery
 behavior must remain passing.

14. Acceptance criteria

Area               The feature is acceptable when...
Simplicity         Only path-relevant questions appear; five-card workflow is clear and fast.
Baseline           Alcohol, tobacco, and work baselines open inline, close after save, and preserve version history.
Weekly data        Alcohol, tobacco, and activity values are quantitative and bound to the correct encounter period.
Status conflict    Former/Never + weekly Yes requires review without discarding weekly answers or auto-reclassifying.
Correct semantics  Zero, No, blank, Unknown, Not applicable, and Declined remain distinct.
Navigation         Save Draft stays; Continue requires completion, commits, then advances.
Recovery           Editable drafts survive logout/login, restart, and date rollover with historical attribution intact.
Security           Main-process authority, trusted IPC, ownership, lifecycle, sanitization, and privacy rules hold.
Offline            Full feature works without internet; no remote dependency is introduced.
Quality            Focused, integration, renderer, regression, migration, build, and visual checks pass.

 Design decisions requiring later explicit approval

q Exact standard-drink conversion guidance for Cameroon and local beverage/container examples.

q Whether secondhand-smoke exposure is included in the first Lifestyle release.

q FHIR resource mapping, recommendations, referral thresholds, and reporting.
